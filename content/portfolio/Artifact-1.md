# Introduction and Description

In the AI Lab activity, I will carry out multiple experiments on AI services and platforms, have conversations with the AI Chatbots and LLMs, create example products with AI services, observe their behavior and output, determine their benefits, and then build an AI chatbot catered to a specific application. 

# Objective

The objective of this activity is to familiarize myself with the latest and greatest AI products and platforms that exist in the world, emphasizing on understanding their pros and cons, what are they good for, and identify any potential use cases for integration and value. 

# Value Proposition

This activity demonstrated my ability to navigate AI services, interact with AI and LLMs, and create a link bewteen what they can do to and what they should do to create value. This is a crucial skill applicable to any project where we want to bring in AI-powered capabilities to a traditional workflow. 

# Process

## Experiment 1: Practice with an LLM

In this experiment, I prompted Claude to create a design for a TV remote as an example project. 

Then, I started 2 other conversations with the same prompt for a total of 3 attempts. 

After the results were generated, I analyzed the similarities and differences between each responses. 

From the observed behaviors, I composed some justifications, and then did some research on the working principles of LLMs to verify my theories.

## Experiment 2: Practice with Custom GPTs

In this experiment, I had a conversation with ChatGPT about the principle of gear reduction. 

First, I asked it to explain how it works. 

Then, I asked for reasons why alternative solutions won't work. 

I also asked for some tangential topics, and questioned some of its answers. 

At last, I asked it to create a summary of the conversation. 

I noted some actions of interest performed by the AI, recorded the places it got the answer right, and where it responded inaccurately.  

Link to the conversation history: https://chatgpt.com/share/6ae58b10-daa8-83e8-af63-cfb3660ca4be

## Experiment 3: Practice with a Research Assistant

Moving away from AI services oriented toward a broad market, I interacted with a research assistant AI for this experiment to investigate its abilities and output. 

The first step was to ask it to generate a research paper on a specific theme, I used "how AC electricity works" as the topic. 

As it was working, I checked the internal activity log, noted what research it was doing, and the sub tasks it chose to perform based on the research results. 

After it generated the paper, I took a brief read, identified the structure of the article, and analyzed the content. 

Link to the generated research paper: [How does AC electricity work_.pdf](/artifact-1/research-paper.pdf)

## Experiment 4: Build a Bot

For this experiment, I utilized Mizou AI platform to create a custom-made AI chatbot targeted toward a single objective. 

Before going to their website and filling boxes, I had to identify the objective of the chatbot, the requirements (what the chatbot should and should not do), the source of data it should reference, how it should engage with the user, and the instructions it should follow. 

I then read the Term of Use of the service, and identified the phrases and caveats relevant for a successful deployment. 

After confirming the requirements of the project and the clauses of the service, I input the parameters to their platform to create the chatbot instance. 

A couple rounds of testing from a user's perspective was done on the resulting chatbot to make sure it behaved acceptably and accomplished its designated tasks. 

The chat bot can be accessed though this link: https://mizou.com/login-thread?ID=0uUx2AclPAu0PnvG7A2a2qLnwDRdsnbtjuYUv371Np8-153627
