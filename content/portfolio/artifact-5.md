# Introduction and Description

In this assignment, me and 4 other members of the Mind Beavers team discussed and presented our findings on how humans and artificial intelligence learn and improve in an iterative manner. 

# Objective

The objective of this assignment was to have a discussion and a presentation about the learning process of both humans and artificial intelligence. 

# Value Proposition

This activity teaches me how artificial intelligence and humans learn and improve, how an iterative approach can bring us forward, and how should we adapt and transform our skills in a world where artificial intelligence can either made us obsolete or accelerate our progress. Additionally, it serves as a challenge for me to collaborate with a team made up of unfamiliar people, to quickly generate ideas, and produce results. 

# Process

## Step 1: Research

The course material provided a good starting point for the research. We read through it first, and then went online to clear up any confusing concepts. Some of us also talked to LLMs to find any related ideas worth exploring. 

## Step 2: Discussion

We then discussed our findings from the research, along with some personal opinions. We agreed that although both humans and artificial intelligence learn in an iterative manner, there are still differences in their behaviors and associated risks. We asked LLMs to give some suggestions about our discussion, just like with another team member, to catch any missing points. 

## Step 3: Composing Content

After the discussion, we worked to compose the slides that we will use for the presentation. It was worth noting that although we did not separate our work into isolated pieces, we collaborated in a well-coordinated manner, so that the contents were coherent, and the slides had a unified appearance, which some other groups lacked. 

## Step 4: Presentation

Only one person presented for each group, but we were all familiar with the content so that any one of us could have done it. Our presentation conveyed the information we had planned, and stayed within timing requirements.
